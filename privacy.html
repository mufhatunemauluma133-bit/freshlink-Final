Privacy Policy

FreshLink Privacy

0. Effective date

Effective date: 31 July 2026. This policy explains what information FreshLink processes and how it is protected.

1. Data collected

FreshLink stores account, listing, job, and message data required to provide platform features. Some preferences are stored locally in your browser.

2. How data is used

Your data is used to authenticate users, display listings, process interactions, and improve app reliability and user support.

3. Sharing

Contact details and listing information are visible to users where required for transactions. FreshLink does not sell personal data.

4. Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards including authenticated access controls, validation and sanitization of user input, and monitored production hardening controls. No internet service can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, but FreshLink continuously improves safeguards to reduce risk.

5. Messaging and contact safety

Message and contact features are provided to enable transactions. Users should avoid sharing passwords, one-time codes, banking PINs, or other secrets in chat or external contact channels.

6. Abuse response

FreshLink may investigate suspicious activity, remove abusive content, and restrict or terminate accounts involved in fraud, impersonation, scraping, or unauthorized access attempts.

7. Your choices

You can update account settings, change password, and clear local application data from the settings page.

8. Contact

For privacy and safety questions, contact the FreshLink support team through official support channels listed in the app settings/support pages.
